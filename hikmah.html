Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Application: Hikmah: Quran, Prayer, Azkar • Effective Date: September 2026 • Developer: OmniLabs

🕌 Spiritual Sanctuary Commitment: Hikmah is engineered as an offline-first spiritual companion. We do not sell your data, track your personal identity, or require accounts to access Islamic content.

1. Information We Access & How It Is Used

  • Location Data (GPS & Network): Your precise or approximate geographic coordinates are accessed strictly on your physical device to compute accurate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and calculate the accurate Qibla compass bearing toward Makkah. Location data is never stored on external servers or transmitted to third parties.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to schedule high-precision Adhan audio reminders and daily Azkar notifications across a 7-day rolling window via Android's AlarmManager.
  • Audio Playback: Used to stream high-definition Quran recitations (Sheikh Alafasy, Sheikh Abdul Basit, Sheikh As-Sudais) and 24/7 live Makkah/Madinah broadcast feeds on-demand.

2. Data Collection & Sharing (Google Play Data Safety)

Hikmah collects zero personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • No user names, email addresses, or phone numbers are collected.
  • No personal device files, photos, or contacts are accessed.
  • No third-party advertising tracking or behavioral telemetry exists on worship surfaces.

3. Offline-First Architecture

The complete Madinah Mushaf (114 Surahs), authentic Sahih International translation, Tafsir commentary, Hisn al-Muslim supplications, and solar prayer calculation algorithms are stored and executed entirely on your smartphone.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Hikmah does not store your data on cloud databases, you retain 100% control over your device settings. Uninstalling the app instantly purges all local bookmarks, custom notes, and calculation preferences.
